Privacy Policy

Support Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Overview

InningPro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.

By using InningPro, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a display name, plus a hashed version of your password if you sign up with email. You can also sign in with Google or Apple. This is used solely to authenticate you and associate your games with your profile.

Game data

When you score or umpire a game, we store game data including scores, play-by-play logs, lineups, and inning details. This data is associated with your account and is used to provide the live game experience and game history.

Teams, leagues and players

Club, league and association managers and scorers can enter team names, player names, jersey numbers, positions, lineups and game results. This data can be seen by anyone who has the game, club or league link or code. Enter only what you are allowed to share.

Web live view

Anyone with a game link can watch a game in a browser, with no account. We do not ask web viewers for personal information. The page is hosted on Firebase Hosting. Joining a game in the app requires an account.

Pitch counts

Pitch counts and rest dates are based on data entered by scorers, and are a guide only.

Usage data and diagnostics

We may collect anonymous crash reports and performance data to improve app stability. This data does not identify you personally.

Advertising

InningPro uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect and use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) to show relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable for InningPro
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

In the EU, UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent choices before showing ads. You can change them under Privacy choices in the app.

For more information on how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Images You Link

Club, league and association logos are links to images hosted elsewhere. InningPro does not store these images. The person who adds a link is responsible for it, and we record who added it and when. When your phone shows a linked image, it loads from that website, which can see your IP address.

Reports and Moderation

You can report content in the app. Reports include the item, any image link, your reason, any details you write and your account ID, and are stored in a private GitHub repository. We may review linked images and reports, and may remove content or disable accounts that break these rules.

Feedback

Feedback you send from the app is stored in a private GitHub repository.

Fonts

To create a box score PDF with Japanese or Chinese text, the app may download a font from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account
  • To store and display your game history
  • To enable real-time game sharing between scorers, umpires, and viewers
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • To serve advertisements via Google AdMob

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your information with third parties except as necessary to operate the app:

  • Firebase (Google): Used for authentication and real-time game data storage. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google AdMob: Used to serve in-app advertisements.
  • GitHub: Used to store feedback and reports.
  • Google Fonts: Used to download fonts for box score PDFs.
  • Websites hosting linked images: Used to load logos that users link.

Data Retention

Your account and game data are retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and its data in the app under Profile, or by contacting us at the email below. We will process email requests within 30 days. Games you created also disappear for everyone who joined them. Results of games in another person's league stay, so that league's standings stay correct, with your name removed.

Young Players

InningPro is for adults who run teams and score games, and is not directed at children under 13, who should not create accounts. Teams may include minors. Adults entering a minor's name are responsible for having permission from a parent or guardian, and should use as little information as needed, such as a first name or jersey number. A parent, guardian or player can ask us to remove this data at the email below, and we will remove it promptly.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • Access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your data
  • Objection to or restriction of processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.
