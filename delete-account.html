How to request account deletion

In the app: open Profile, tap Delete account and confirm it's you. Deletion starts right away.

By email: if you can't use the app, you can request deletion of your InningPro account and all associated data by sending us an email. We will process your request within 30 days.

  1. Send an email to avidity.wands.70@icloud.com with the subject line "Delete my InningPro account".
  2. Include the email address associated with your InningPro account.
  3. We will confirm receipt and process your deletion request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  1. Account information — your email address and authentication credentials
  2. Game data — all games you created, including scores, play-by-play logs, and lineups. They also disappear for everyone who joined them.
  3. Clubs, leagues and associations — the ones you manage, including rosters and join requests
  4. Joined game history — records of games you participated in as scorer, umpire, or viewer

What is retained

  • Games in another person's league: results and lineups stay, so that league's standings stay correct. Your name is removed from the game's participants.
  • Other people's games: your account ID can stay in the play log of games you scored. It no longer links to an account.
  • Reports and feedback: what you sent stays in our private repository. Contact us to have it removed.

Aggregated, anonymised statistics that do not identify you personally may be retained for service improvement purposes.
