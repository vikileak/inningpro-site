Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do viewers need an account? No — fans can watch a live game by scanning a QR code, no sign-up required.
  • How many scorers can join a game? Games support multiple scorers and umpires joining the same live session.
  • Is the app free? InningPro is free to download. Some features may be supported by ads.
  • What platforms is InningPro available on? InningPro is available on iPhone, iPad, and Android.
  • Can I review previous games? Yes — completed games are saved to your history with a full play-by-play log.
